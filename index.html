Got Questions?

A few things you might want to know.

Is this platform free?

The goal of SSC Practice Hub is to provide free practice resources without putting essential preparation behind a paywall.

How do I use the Telegram bot?

Open @anu_ssc_bot on Telegram and press Start. The bot will gradually provide practice features as the system is developed.

Which SSC exams is this useful for?

The platform is being built around SSC CHSL, CGL, MTS, CPO and other major SSC exam patterns.

Will the questions be actual PYQs?

PYQs will be clearly identified. Fresh practice questions will be labelled separately so you can always know what type of question you are solving.
