Our Purpose

SIKSHORA is a competitive examination preparation platform built to make learning and practice more organised and accessible. The goal is to bring useful question practice and learning tools together in one simple experience.

Our Direction

We are starting with a focused question bank and building towards wider exam coverage, better progress tracking and more helpful preparation features over time.

Frequently asked questions

Some quick answers about the SIKSHORA platform.

What is SIKSHORA?

SIKSHORA is a competitive examination preparation platform focused on question practice and learning resources.

Which exams are supported right now?

The platform is being developed with SSC-first question coverage. Other exam categories are part of the broader roadmap and should not be treated as fully available yet.

How do I start practising?

Select the Start Practicing button to open the practice dashboard. Choose from the exams and question sets currently available.

Are Hindi and English both available?

Hindi and English support is being developed. Language availability depends on the translations available in each question set.

Can I use SIKSHORA on my phone?

Yes. The website is designed to adjust to mobile, tablet and desktop screen sizes.

Will my progress sync across devices?

Account-based progress syncing across devices is planned and will become available after the backend is ready.
